# Sales call 2 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-08-27
- Company: dental clinic group in Abuja. Three branches, 22 staff.
- On the call: the group's operations manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-09-10

**Account executive:** What's the main problem you want to solve?

**Operations manager:** About one patient in five just doesn't turn up, and we've already turned someone else away for that slot.

**Account executive:** What brought it to a head?

**Operations manager:** Our new finance director asked why so much chair time is empty. Nobody had an answer.

**Account executive:** Have you tried anything for it?

**Operations manager:** We tried one of the big international scheduling apps for two months. Billing in dollars on a card was a problem, and patients don't read email reminders.

**Account executive:** So what made you choose us?

**Operations manager:** The reminders go out on WhatsApp, which patients actually read, and we pay in naira by transfer.

**Account executive:** Who makes the final decision?

**Operations manager:** The medical director signs. I run the trial and recommend.
