# Sales call 3 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-09-03
- Company: dental clinic in Ikeja, Lagos. One branch, 18 staff, six dentists.
- On the call: the clinic's practice manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-09-15

**Account executive:** Tell me about the front desk.

**Practice manager:** When the front desk is busy with a patient, the phone just rings out. We know people give up.

**Account executive:** Anything else going wrong?

**Practice manager:** Twice last month two dentists were booked into the same surgery at the same time. We run everything off a big paper diary.

**Account executive:** Have you tried adding staff?

**Practice manager:** We advertised for a receptionist three times since March and couldn't keep anyone.

**Account executive:** What made you decide on us?

**Practice manager:** One diary for all six dentists, and patients book themselves on WhatsApp, so the phone matters less.

**Account executive:** Who approves the spend?

**Practice manager:** The owner. I prepare the case and she signs.
