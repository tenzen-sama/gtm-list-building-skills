# Sales call 6 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-10-14
- Company: dental clinic in Port Harcourt. Two branches, 12 staff.
- On the call: the clinic administrator; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-10-28

**Account executive:** Why now?

**Clinic administrator:** Patients complain online that they can't get through to us. And on a bad week a quarter of the bookings don't show.

**Account executive:** What have you tried?

**Clinic administrator:** Our first idea was to hire another receptionist. It would cost about the same and still not cover the evenings.

**Account executive:** What decided it?

**Clinic administrator:** The automatic reminders, and patients can rebook themselves without calling.

**Account executive:** Who signs?

**Clinic administrator:** The two partners. I recommend, they sign.
