# Sales call 1 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-08-12
- Company: dental clinic in Lekki, Lagos. Two branches, 14 staff.
- On the call: the clinic's practice manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-08-20

**Account executive:** What made you start looking?

**Practice manager:** We opened the second branch in June and the phones fell apart. After four o'clock nobody picks up, and patients just call the next clinic.

**Account executive:** How do you know you're losing them?

**Practice manager:** Patients left reviews saying we never answer the phone. Two in one month. That hurt.

**Account executive:** What were you using before?

**Practice manager:** A paper diary at each front desk and the receptionists' own WhatsApp. Nothing joined up.

**Account executive:** Did you look at anything else?

**Practice manager:** Not really. We thought about hiring another receptionist, but that only fixes one branch.

**Account executive:** What made you choose us?

**Practice manager:** Patients can book on WhatsApp themselves, and I can see both branches in one diary.

**Account executive:** Who signs off on this?

**Practice manager:** The owner signs. He's the principal dentist. I take it to him with the numbers.
