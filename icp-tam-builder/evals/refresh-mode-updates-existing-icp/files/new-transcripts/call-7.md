# Sales call 7 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-10-20
- Company: dental clinic in Surulere, Lagos. Two branches, 16 staff.
- On the call: the clinic's practice manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-10-29

**Account executive:** What's been happening at the front desk?

**Practice manager:** Patients ring at lunchtime and nobody's free to answer. By the afternoon there are a dozen missed calls.

**Account executive:** What were you planning to do about it?

**Practice manager:** We were going to hire a second receptionist, but that only covers one desk.

**Account executive:** What made you choose us instead?

**Practice manager:** WhatsApp booking means the phone stops being the only way in.

**Account executive:** Who signs?

**Practice manager:** The owner. I bring it to her with the numbers.
