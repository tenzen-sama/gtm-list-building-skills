# Sales call 8 (fictional, for testing)

- Date of call: 2026-10-30
- Company: dental clinic group in Wuse, Abuja. Three branches, 25 staff.
- On the call: the group's operations manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-11-12

**Account executive:** What's the main problem?

**Operations manager:** Patients don't turn up, and the branches double-book the same dentist because nobody can see the other diaries.

**Account executive:** How do you manage the diaries today?

**Operations manager:** A shared Google Sheet between the branches. Someone always forgets to update it.

**Account executive:** Why us?

**Operations manager:** One diary for all three branches, and the reminders go out on WhatsApp without anyone remembering to send them.

**Account executive:** Who makes the decision?

**Operations manager:** The medical director signs. I run the trial.
