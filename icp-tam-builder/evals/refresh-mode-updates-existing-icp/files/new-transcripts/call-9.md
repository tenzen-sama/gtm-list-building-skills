- Date of call: 2026-11-06
- Company: dental clinic in GRA, Port Harcourt. Two branches, 11 staff.
- On the call: the clinic's practice manager; an account executive from Example Booking
- Outcome: won, signed 2026-11-13

**Practice manager:** Patients call after we close and nobody answers, so they book with whoever picks up.

**Account executive:** What changed your mind?

**Practice manager:** Patients can book at night on WhatsApp, and it's there in the diary when we open.

**Account executive:** Who signs?

**Practice manager:** The two partners sign. I make the recommendation.
